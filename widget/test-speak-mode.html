✨ New Speak Mode Features:

  • Voice Conversation: Click the "Speak" tab to enter voice mode
  • Tap to Speak: Click the microphone button to start speaking
  • AI Voice Response: The AI will respond with text-to-speech
  • Visual Feedback: Animated waveforms show when listening/speaking
  • Conversation Transcript: See a log of your voice conversation
  • Easy Switch: Toggle between Chat and Speak modes anytime
⚠️ Note: Voice features require browser support. Works best in Chrome, Edge, and Safari. Make sure to allow microphone permissions when prompted.
